Posters / Poster P25

Quantum Materials for Axion Dark Matter Detection

Jaanita Mehrani

Graduate Student, Applied Physics Graduate Program, Rice University
Advisor: Prof. Shengxi Huang and Prof. Junichiro Kono

Lunch and Poster Session · Day 2 · Dec 4 · 12:15 pm – 2:30 pm

Abstract

Jaanita Mehrani

This work presents a novel tabletop dark matter detector for the direct detection of meV axions, a dark matter candidate which can additionally solve the strong charge-party problem in quantum chromodynamics. The detection mechanism relies on the inverse Primakoff effect, where axions convert to photons in a strong magnetic field. We propose designer quantum semiconductor heterostructures to resonantly enhance the signal. By embedding high-mobility two-dimensional electron gases into multiple quantum wells, Semiconductor-Quantum-Well Axion Radiometer Experiments (SQWAREs) form magnetoplasmonic cavities featuring tunable epsilon-near-zero resonances in the THz range, which correspond to sensitivity to meV mass axions. Dynamically adjusting the cavity orientation relative to the applied magnetic field allows in situ tuning of both the resonant frequency and the radiatively emitted electromagnetic signal, facilitating efficient sweeps across target axion mass windows. We present the theoretical foundations and project the sensitivity of a few optimistic SQWARE configurations as well as present current experimental research and development, highlighting the platform's adaptability to existing laboratory setups and ability to bridge a critical coverage gap for meV-scale axion searches.
