Posters / Poster P19

Linewidth narrowing of device-coupled single T centers via above-band optical pumping

Adam Johnston

Graduate Student, Applied Physics Graduate Program, Rice University
Advisor: Prof. Songtao Chen

Lunch and Poster Session · Day 2 · Dec 4 · 12:15 pm – 2:30 pm

Abstract

Adam Johnston

The silicon T center is a promising candidate to underpin future quantum networking schemes due to its telecom O-band spin-photon interface [1]. However, T centers often exhibit broad optical linewidths when coupled to photonic devices due to spectral diffusion [2], [3]. We investigate the nature of this spectral diffusion using an above-band optical pumping technique and report a 57% reduction in the single T-center linewidth.

The narrowing effect of the above-band laser is attributed to a saturation of the occupancy of silicon charge traps [4], whose fluctuation leads to spectral diffusion. Furthermore, we investigate the time dynamics of this effect, revealing a lower-bound 300 μs duration for the stability of the T-center linewidth in the dark. We develop a model to explain the dynamics of the laser's effect on the trap occupancy, the results point towards strategies for further narrowing the T-center linewidth. Moreover, the work provides a method for narrowing single T centers, clearing the path for their use in future quantum networking experiments.

  3. C. Bowness et al., “Laser-Induced Spectral Diffusion and Excited-State Mixing of Silicon T Centers,” PRX Quantum, vol. 6, no. 3, p. 030350, Sep. 2025.
