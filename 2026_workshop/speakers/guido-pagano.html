Guido Pagano

Associate Professor of Physics and Astronomy
Department of Physics and Astronomy, Rice University

Short bio

Guido Pagano is an Associate Professor of Physics and Astronomy at Rice University. He received undergraduate degrees in economics from Università Bocconi (2006) and in physics from the Università degli Studi di Milano (2009), and a master's degree in physics from Sapienza University of Rome (2011). He earned his Ph.D. in 2015 at the Scuola Normale Superiore in Pisa and LENS in Florence. From 2015 to 2018 he was an Intelligence Community Postdoctoral Fellow at the Joint Quantum Institute and the University of Maryland with Christopher Monroe, and then a research scientist there until 2019. After a visiting appointment at Oxford University in 2019, he joined Rice as an Assistant Professor in 2020. His awards include an NSF CAREER Award (2022), an ONR Young Investigator Award (2022), a DOE Early Career Award (2023), and the Rice School of Natural Sciences Excellence in Undergraduate Mentoring Award (2025).

Research areas

  • Trapped-ion quantum simulation
  • Neutral-atom quantum systems
  • Quantum many-body physics
  • Open quantum systems and electron transfer simulation
