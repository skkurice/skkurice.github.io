Sang-Hee Shin

Assistant Professor
Department of Quantum Information Engineering, Sungkyunkwan University

Short bio

Sang-Hee Shin received an M.Eng. in 2018 and a Ph.D. in electrical and electronic engineering in 2022 from Imperial College London. From 2022 to 2024 he was a Higher Research Scientist at the National Physical Laboratory (NPL) in the United Kingdom, and since 2022 he has been a visiting researcher at Imperial College London. He was an assistant professor at Sejong University from 2024 to 2025 and joined the Department of Quantum Information Engineering at Sungkyunkwan University as an assistant professor in September 2025. His research covers the design and manufacture of RF and quasi-optical devices for microwave, millimeter-wave, and terahertz applications, on-wafer RF measurements, and high-frequency metrology for cryogenic quantum computing.

Research areas

  • RF and microwave engineering for superconducting quantum computing
  • Cryogenic RF measurement and metrology
  • Millimeter-wave and terahertz devices
  • On-wafer S-parameter measurement and calibration
  • 3-D printed waveguide and quasi-optical components
