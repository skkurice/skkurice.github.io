Shengxi Huang

Associate Professor of Electrical and Computer Engineering and of Materials Science and NanoEngineering
Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, Rice University

Short bio

Shengxi Huang received a B.S. in micro and nano electronics from Tsinghua University in 2011, and an M.S. (2013) and Ph.D. (2017) in electrical engineering and computer science from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology. She was a postdoctoral researcher at Stanford University with Tony Heinz and Jonathan Fan, and then an assistant professor of electrical engineering, biomedical engineering, and the Materials Research Institute at Penn State University. She is now an associate professor of electrical and computer engineering and of materials science and nanoengineering at Rice University, where she leads the SCOPE Lab. Her honors include an AFOSR Young Investigator Award (2022), an NSF CAREER Award (2020), a Johnson & Johnson STEM2D Scholar's Award (2019), the MIT Jin Au Kong Award for Best PhD Thesis (2017), a Ginzton Fellowship at Stanford (2017), and a Kavli Fellowship for Nanoscience (2017).

Research areas

  • Low-dimensional materials
  • Light-matter interactions and Raman spectroscopy
  • Weyl semimetals
  • Quantum optical emission and quantum sensing
  • Chemical and biological sensing
