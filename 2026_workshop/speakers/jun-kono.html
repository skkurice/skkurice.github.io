Jun Kono

Karl F. Hasselmann Chair in Engineering; Professor of Electrical and Computer Engineering; Director, Smalley-Curl Institute
Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, Rice University

Short bio

Junichiro Kono received his B.S. (1990) and M.S. (1992) in applied physics from the University of Tokyo and his Ph.D. in physics from the State University of New York at Buffalo in 1995. He was a postdoctoral associate at the University of California, Santa Barbara from 1995 to 1997 and a W. W. Hansen Experimental Physics Laboratory Fellow at Stanford University from 1997 to 2000. He joined Rice University in 2000 as an assistant professor and became a professor in 2009. He holds the Karl F. Hasselmann Chair in Engineering and is director of the Smalley-Curl Institute. His honors include the Frank Isakson Prize for Optical Effects in Solids of the American Physical Society, an NSF CAREER Award (2002), and fellowships of the APS (2009), OSA (2015), SPIE (2019), IOP (2021), and JSAP (2023).

Research areas

  • Quantum optical phenomena in solids
  • Ultrastrong light-matter coupling
  • Terahertz science and devices
  • Semiconductor optics
  • Carbon nanomaterials
