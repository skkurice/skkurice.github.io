Seunghyun Baik

Professor
School of Mechanical Engineering, Sungkyunkwan University

Short bio

Seunghyun Baik received a B.S. (1995) and an M.S. (1997) in mechanical engineering from Yonsei University and a Ph.D. in mechanical engineering from the University of Wisconsin-Madison in 2001. From 2001 to 2004 he was a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Chemical and Biomolecular Engineering at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign. He joined Sungkyunkwan University in 2004 as an assistant professor, became an associate professor in 2010, and has been a professor in the School of Mechanical Engineering since 2016. He served as vice dean of the College of Engineering (2021 to 2022) and chair of the School of Mechanical Engineering (2023 to 2024). He is an editor of the Journal of Mechanical Science and Technology and has served on the editorial board of Energy Storage Materials. His honors include the Korean Society of Mechanical Engineers Academic Award (2020) and the SKKU Young Fellowship.

Research areas

  • Electrically and thermally conductive nanocomposites
  • Thermal interface materials and thermal management
  • Thermal rectification
  • Carbon nanomaterials and metal nanoparticles
  • Stretchable conductors and composite sensors
