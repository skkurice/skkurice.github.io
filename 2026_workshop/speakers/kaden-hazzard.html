Kaden Hazzard

Associate Professor of Physics and Astronomy
Department of Physics and Astronomy, Rice University

Short bio

Kaden Hazzard is an Associate Professor of Physics and Astronomy at Rice University, where he also serves as Director of the Rice Quantum Initiative and Chair of the Graduate Program in Physics and Astronomy. He received a B.S. in physics and mathematics from The Ohio State University in 2004, and an M.S. (2009) and a Ph.D. (2010) in physics from Cornell University, where he worked with Erich Mueller. From 2010 to 2014 he worked with Ana Maria Rey at JILA (NIST and the University of Colorado Boulder) as a research associate, NRC postdoctoral fellow, and senior research associate. He joined Rice as an Assistant Professor in 2014 and became an Associate Professor in 2021. His honors include an NSF CAREER Award (2019), Fellowship of the American Physical Society (2023), and the Charles W. Duncan, Jr. Achievement Award from Rice (2024).

Research areas

  • Theoretical atomic, molecular and optical physics
  • Quantum many-body theory
  • Quantum matter
  • Quantum computing
