Emilia Morosan

William M. Rice Trustee Professor of Physics and Astronomy; Director, Rice Center for Quantum Materials
Department of Physics and Astronomy, Rice University

Short bio

Emilia Morosan received a B.S. in physics from Alexandru Ioan Cuza University in Romania in 1999 and a Ph.D. in physics from Iowa State University in 2005, with doctoral work at Ames Laboratory. She joined Princeton University as a postdoctoral associate in chemistry in 2005 and has been a member of the Rice University faculty since 2007. She is a professor of physics and astronomy, chemistry, and materials science and nanoengineering, was named William M. Rice Trustee Professor in 2025, and is director of the Rice Center for Quantum Materials and founding director of Q-RaMP, a partnership between Rice and the Max Planck Society. Her group designs and synthesizes quantum materials with emergent properties. Her honors include a Presidential Early Career Award for Scientists and Engineers, an NSF CAREER Award, selection as a Gordon and Betty Moore Foundation EPiQS Investigator, and a 2024 Vannevar Bush Faculty Fellowship.

Research areas

  • Design and synthesis of quantum materials
  • Topological semimetals and magnetic topological materials
  • Superconductivity and density waves
  • Kondo materials
  • Crystal growth (flux, vapor transport, solid state)
