Hosung Seo

Associate Professor
Department of Quantum Information Engineering, Sungkyunkwan University

Short bio

Associate Professor in the Department of Quantum Information Engineering at Sungkyunkwan University, with a joint appointment in SAINT and chair of the graduate Department of Quantum Engineering. His group uses first-principles theory and computation to understand and design quantum information materials and devices, with projects on diamond for quantum networks and sensing and on quantum coherence and entanglement.
