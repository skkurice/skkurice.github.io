Hosung Seo

Associate Professor
Department of Quantum Information Engineering, Sungkyunkwan University

Short bio

Hosung Seo received a B.S. in physics from Seoul National University in 2006, an M.S. in physics from Northwestern University in 2008, and a Ph.D. in physics from The University of Texas at Austin in 2013. He was a postdoctoral fellow at the University of California, Davis (2013 to 2014), the University of Chicago (2014 to 2017), and Argonne National Laboratory (2016 to 2017). From 2017 to 2024 he was an assistant and then associate professor in the Department of Physics at Ajou University and spent 2023 to 2024 as a visiting professor at the University of Wisconsin-Madison. In 2024 he joined the Department of Quantum Information Engineering at Sungkyunkwan University as an associate professor. He also chairs the graduate Department of Quantum Engineering and is an affiliated professor in the SKKU Advanced Institute of Nanotechnology. He received the William Powers, Jr. Presidential Fellowship from UT Austin in 2012.

Research areas

  • Electronic structure theory
  • Solid-state physics
  • Open quantum systems and decoherence
