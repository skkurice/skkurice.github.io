Youngkuk Kim

Associate Professor
Department of Physics, Sungkyunkwan University

Short bio

Youngkuk Kim received a B.S. in physics and mathematics (dual major) from Seoul National University in 2006 and a Ph.D. in condensed matter theory from Seoul National University in 2013, advised by Jisoon Ihm. From 2013 to 2017 he was a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Pennsylvania, in the Department of Chemistry and the LRSM topological insulator seed program. He joined the Department of Physics at Sungkyunkwan University as an assistant professor in 2017 and has been an associate professor since 2021. His group uses first-principles calculations and effective theories to study topological and strongly correlated quantum materials, connecting symmetry, topology, quantum geometry, and electronic correlations with measurable phenomena.

Research areas

  • Theoretical condensed matter physics
  • First-principles calculations
  • Topological materials
  • Strongly correlated materials
  • Quantum geometry in two-dimensional materials
