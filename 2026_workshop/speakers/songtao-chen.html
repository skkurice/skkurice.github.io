Songtao Chen

Assistant Professor of Electrical and Computer Engineering
Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, Rice University

Short bio

Songtao Chen is an Assistant Professor of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Rice University and a member of the Ken Kennedy Institute. He received a B.S. in optical science and engineering from Zhejiang University in 2013, and an M.S. (2016) and a Ph.D. (2018) in electrical and computer engineering from Brown University. From 2018 to 2021 he was a postdoctoral research associate in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Princeton University, where he worked on multiplexed quantum network nodes using single erbium ions. He joined Rice in July 2021 as part of the Rice University Quantum Initiative. His group studies the control of defect spins in silicon with telecom optical interfaces for scalable quantum networks. His honors include an NSF CAREER Award (2023), the Outstanding Thesis Award from Brown University (2018), and a William Prager Fellowship at Brown University (2014).
