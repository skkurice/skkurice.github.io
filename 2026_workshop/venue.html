Venue

Rice University, 6100 Main Street, Houston, TX 77005, USA (building and room to be announced)

Getting there

From George Bush Intercontinental Airport (IAH)
IAH is Houston's main international airport, north of the city. The drive to Rice University takes about 30–60 minutes, depending on traffic.
From William P. Hobby Airport (HOU)
HOU is Houston's second airport, southeast of downtown and closer to campus. The drive to Rice University takes about 20–30 minutes.
Taxi or rideshare
Taxis, Uber, and Lyft are available at both airports. Use the address Rice University, 6100 Main Street.
Public transit
METRO buses run from both airports to downtown Houston. From downtown, the METRORail Red Line runs along Main Street and stops across from the main entrance of the Rice campus. Check current routes and times at ridemetro.org.
