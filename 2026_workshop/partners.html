Academic Partners

Sungkyunkwan University

  • College of Engineering

    The College of Engineering at SKKU offers undergraduate and graduate programs across engineering.

  • College of Science

    The College of Science consists of four undergraduate departments (Physics, Life Sciences, Mathematics, and Chemistry) and six graduate programs, with a focus on basic science.

  • Department of Physics

    Physics research at SKKU covers a broad range of topics, with main areas in particle and nuclear physics, statistical and condensed-matter physics, and nano and biophysics.

  • Department of Quantum Information Engineering (Undergraduate) & Department of Quantum Engineering (Graduate)

    The department was established as the first undergraduate program in Korea specializing in quantum information science and technology.

  • School of Mechanical Engineering

    The School of Mechanical Engineering was formed in 1996 by integrating the Department of Mechanical Engineering, founded in 1966, and the Department of Machine Design, established in 1978.

Rice University

  • Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering

    Electrical and Computer Engineering at Rice works across hardware and software, with research in computing, communications, electronics, photonics, and healthcare.

  • Department of Physics and Astronomy

    The Department of Physics and Astronomy studies the fundamental laws and organizing principles of the natural world, from elementary particles and ultracold atomic gases to the universe.

  • Rice Center for Quantum Materials

    The Rice Center for Quantum Materials seeks to sustain and grow fundamental research on quantum materials at Rice and to develop an international network in this area.

  • Rice Quantum Initiative

    The Rice Quantum Initiative is a multidisciplinary community working toward computation, sensing, and communication technologies based on the principles of quantum mechanics.

  • Smalley-Curl Institute

    The Smalley-Curl Institute supports research and education in nanoscale science and technology, quantum materials, and quantum information science, with faculty from across science and engineering at Rice.

  • Wiess School of Natural Sciences

    The Wiess School of Natural Sciences at Rice includes departments of biosciences, chemistry, earth, environmental and planetary sciences, kinesiology, mathematics, and physics and astronomy.
