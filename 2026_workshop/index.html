About the workshop

The SKKU⁠–⁠Rice Quantum Collaboration Workshop brings together researchers from Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU) and Rice University who work on quantum science and technology, with a focus on quantum information, quantum materials, nanomaterials, and devices.

Topics include condensed-matter theory and open quantum systems; trapped-ion and superconducting-circuit experiments; RF and microwave technologies for quantum computing; atomic, molecular, and optical (AMO) physics; quantum materials and nanomaterials; and quantum error correction.

The program consists of invited talks by faculty members from both universities and a poster session by graduate students. The workshop aims to share recent work and to identify opportunities for collaboration.
